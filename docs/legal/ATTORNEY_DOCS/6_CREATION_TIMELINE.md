## YOUR SIMPLE EXPLANATION

**Why does the attorney need a timeline?**
In patent law, the person who invented something FIRST has the stronger claim. A timeline with real dates proves when you built each part. Your git commit history (the automatic log your code system keeps) is legal evidence of when things were created.

**What a "commit" is:** Every time you save a major version of your code, the system stamps it with the date and a description. It's like a receipt for every piece of work you did. You have 77+ of these receipts.

**The key message:** "I have a documented, timestamped development history starting June 3, 2026, with 77+ commits across the frontend and backend."
