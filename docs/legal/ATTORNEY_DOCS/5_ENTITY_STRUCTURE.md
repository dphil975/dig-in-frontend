## YOUR SIMPLE EXPLANATION

**What is an "entity"?**
An entity is the official legal company that owns your app. Right now, as a solo person building an app, YOU personally own everything. But when you raise money or hire people, you need a proper company set up — usually a "C-Corporation" in Delaware — so the company owns the IP, not just you personally.

**What to tell the attorney:** "I haven't formed a company yet. I'm a sole inventor. I want to know what I need to set up and in what order before I talk to investors."

**Why this matters:** If you form your company AFTER filing a patent, the patent might be in your personal name and you'll need to assign (transfer) it to the company later. It's cleaner to do it in the right order. Your attorney will tell you which comes first.
