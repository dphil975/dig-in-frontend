### Simple version just for you · Attorney meeting · July 2026

## What You're Going to Say (Practice This Out Loud)

### When they say "Tell me about your app" — say this:

"I built an app called Dig In. It's a food app with two sides.

The first side is for regular people. They open the app, see a map of grocery stores near them, check in when they visit a store, and earn coins. Those coins unlock outfits for a little pixel art food character they can create. The app also suggests recipes based on what's nearby and lets people track what they've cooked.

The second side is for businesses. Every time someone uses the app, it quietly collects anonymous information — what neighborhood they're in, what store they visited, what ingredients are trending. We package that information into reports and sell them to grocery stores and food brands. They use it to understand what shoppers in their area actually want.

The consumer side makes the business side possible. More users = better data = more valuable reports."

---

## Five Things Your App Does (Know These Cold)

**Thing 1 — The Map + Check-In**
People see stores on a map. They tap a store, check in, and earn coins. This is what makes people open the app every day.

**Thing 2 — Data Collection (The Business Part)**
Every check-in quietly saves anonymous info: what zip code, what store, what time. This gets packaged and sold to grocery stores as a report. Think of it like a weather report, but for shopping behavior.

**Thing 3 — Recipe Suggestions**
The app has over 600 recipes from 70+ countries. It suggests recipes based on what stores are nearby or what ingredients you have.

**Thing 4 — The Substitution Engine**
If you're missing an ingredient, the app tells you what to use instead. It has 300+ substitution rules (example: no buttermilk? use 1 cup milk + 1 tablespoon lemon juice, wait 5 minutes).

**Thing 5 — Pixel Art Creator**
Users design a little food character using pixel art. They can unlock outfits with the coins they earn from checking in. This is what makes the app fun and shareable.

## Words That Might Come Up — Simple Definitions

| Word | What It Actually Means |
|------|----------------------|
| **Patent** | Government permission that says only YOU can use this specific invention for 20 years |
| **Provisional patent** | A cheaper, faster version that gives you 12 months of protection while you figure out the full patent |
| **Prior art** | Someone else already did something similar before you |
| **Copyright** | Automatic protection on your code and designs — you already have this |
| **Trade secret** | A secret process you never tell anyone (like Coca-Cola's recipe) |
| **Trademark** | Protecting your brand name "Dig In" so nobody else can use it |
| **Inventorship** | Who officially invented it — that's you |
| **IP** | Intellectual Property — all the things you created that have value |
| **Entity / C-Corp** | The official legal company you'll set up (like "Dig In Inc.") |
| **Priority date** | The official date you first created this — earlier is better |
