# Dig In — Pre-Launch Content Pack

**Positioning line (use this everywhere):**
> Dig In is the hub for the part of adulthood nobody taught you. What to eat, where to go, how to host, how to not get overcharged for it. One app instead of eleven.

**Secondary line (softer, for Reels VO):**
> I didn't want more options. I wanted fewer decisions.

**Stage:** pre-launch. Every piece ends in waitlist, not download.
**CTA:** `Waitlist is in my bio. It's small on purpose.`

## PART 3 — Posting Order & The Listening System

### 4-week sequence (3 posts/week, Tue / Thu / Sun 7pm)

| Week | Tue | Thu | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reel 1** (4PM Question) | Caption 5 (Health) | **Reel 6** (Tell Me What's Broken) |
| 2 | **Reel 2** (The Binder) | Caption 2 (Money) | **Reel 5** (Sunday, Nine People) |
| 3 | **Reel 3** ($47 Salad) | Caption 6 (Boredom) | Caption 4 (Community) |
| 4 | **Reel 4** (Eleven Apps) | Caption 7 (Mom / Easter egg) | Caption 10 (Waitlist push) |

Reel 1 goes first because it's the widest problem. Reel 6 goes on day three while the new followers are still warm and will actually type.

### The listening system (this is the part most brands skip)

You said it yourself: brands fail because they don't listen to complaints. So make listening a literal file, not a vibe.

**Weekly, 30 minutes, Sunday morning before you cook:**

1. Copy every comment and DM that describes a *problem* into one doc. Verbatim. Don't paraphrase — paraphrasing is where you accidentally replace their problem with yours.
2. Tag each one: `FOOD` / `MONEY` / `TRAVEL` / `HOSTING` / `TIME` / `SHAME`
3. Count the tags. The top tag next week is the feature you build and the Reel you script.
4. Reply to the person whose complaint you're building from. By name. "I'm building this. It's yours." — that person becomes a founding user and tells everyone.

**The flywheel:** their complaint → your Reel → their comment "this is literally me" → your DM → their waitlist signup → your beta tester. Same person, four steps.

**Stories, 3x/week, low effort:**
- Poll: "the worst part of feeding yourself is…" (4 options, rotate them)
- Question box: "what did you eat last night, no judgment" — this is free market research and people love answering it
- Quiet build-in-public: one screenshot, one sentence, no hype

### Easter eggs to keep seeding
- The green tab, third from the front — mom
- The frog appears in every kitchen shot. Never mention it until someone asks. Then tell the story once.
- Nine chairs, always nine. Someone will count.
- You never say the app name in the first 10 seconds of any Reel. It's a reward for staying.
