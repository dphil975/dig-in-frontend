🥘 Ingredient Grid System

Mapo Tofu

Silky tofu in spicy Sichuan sauce

⏰ 30 min
🍽️ 4 servings
⭐ Medium
💡 Interactive: Click the ✓ or ✗ button on any ingredient to toggle between "have" and "don't have". The ingredient box will update its appearance and show as faded when missing. Items marked as missing will be removed from your shopping needs!
📖 Recipe Steps Slider
💡 Interactive: Click the arrow buttons to navigate between steps, or click the dots at the bottom to jump to a specific step. The step card will smoothly transition, and you can see your progress with the numbered indicator and counter.
💖 Item Modification Card (Love Shot Style)
💡 Features: Adjust quantity with +/− buttons, select options from the dropdown, and see the 💲 price modifier update automatically. Add items to your recipe with custom modifications!
🎬 Profile History Reel
💡 Feature: Scroll horizontally through your saved recipes. Each card shows the recipe name, type, ingredient preview (4 key items as pixel emojis), cooking time, servings, and quick-view button. This reel displays your complete cooking history!
