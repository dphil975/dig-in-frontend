👃 Flavor Science Learning Path

1

Master the Five Tastes

Learn sweet, salty, sour, bitter, and umami. Understand what each taste indicates and which foods contain them.

2

Understand Smell-Flavor Connection

Discover that 80-90% of flavor comes from smell, not taste buds. Learn orthonasal and retronasal olfaction.

3

Learn Flavor Pairing Principles

Apply science-based rules: salt inhibits bitter, acid balances richness, umami enhances satisfaction, fat carries flavor.

4

Study Cooking Reactions

Learn how Maillard reaction, caramelization, and umami development create complex flavors through cooking methods.

5

Develop Your Palate

Practice tasting techniques. Analyze flavors systematically. Apply principles to your own cooking for immediate improvements.
